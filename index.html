Response timing Recommended defaults
0.8 s

Recommended: 0.8 seconds. The app waits after the latest finalized speech before accepting a complete question. Resumed speech postpones it. Shorter waits feel quicker but may split a mid-thought pause; longer waits add delay. Transcription and network time are additional.

6.5 s

Recommended: 6.5 seconds. A prefix such as “Can you explain…” stays open for the rest of the question. A short window may lose a delayed continuation. An unfinished prefix expires without requesting an answer. This does not delay an already complete question.

75 s

Recommended: 75 seconds. The maximum total time from accepting a question through connection refresh and answer streaming. Lowering this does not make the provider think faster: it cancels sooner and may leave a partial answer. No paid retry is made automatically.
